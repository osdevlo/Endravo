Offline Maps For Apple Watch

Create the route on your wrist. Keep navigating beyond the signal.

Download an area from Endravo on iPhone, then create a route directly on Apple Watch or follow one you already saved. Roads, trails, position, planned and covered paths, distance, and turn cues remain available when cellular service disappears.

01 Choose an area Select the part of the map you need from the Endravo iPhone app.
02 Save it to Apple Watch Download the map and its routing data to your Watch before leaving reliable coverage.
03 Create and navigate Place destinations and choose one-way, return-loop, or out-and-back routing from the Watch.
Get Endravo

Download map areas before leaving coverage. GPS remains available without cellular service. Offline maps are a supplemental navigation aid and do not replace official maps, trail signage, or sound judgment.
