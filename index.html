Planning Flow

From training intent to a week you can execute.

Start with the goal, shape the schedule, route the run, send the workout to Apple Watch, then review what actually happened. Endravo keeps that loop in one place.

01 Choose the training focus: race prep, base building, strength, recovery, or weight management. Goal
02 Build the week with runs, strength sessions, recovery work, and planned intensity. Schedule
03 Prepare routes and workouts so the session is ready before you leave. Prepare
04 Review completed activity and recovery context on iPhone or the dashboard. Review
