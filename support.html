Compatible with Strava

Your activities. Your permission.

Endravo is an independent app. It is not developed, sponsored, or endorsed by Strava.

Connect Endravo to Strava
  1. In Endravo, open Profile → Data & Permissions → Strava.
  2. Choose Connect with Strava. Sign in on Strava’s authorization page, review the requested permissions, and choose Authorize.
  3. Return to Endravo and check that the connection is shown as connected.

Endravo uses Strava’s authorization flow; you do not give your Strava password to Endravo. The connection requests profile access and permission to create or update your activities (read and activity:write).

What is sent, received, and stored?

With your permission, Endravo sends the activity title, caption, sport, date, duration, distance, and GPS route when available. Strava returns your connected athlete identifier/profile information and upload results. Endravo stores connection credentials on its backend and activity upload records to show status and prevent duplicate submissions.

Connecting does not import your entire Strava activity history. Activity visibility is controlled in Strava; review your Strava privacy settings before uploading. See our Strava privacy disclosure.

Automatic uploads, older activities, and duplicates

New activities recorded after connecting are eligible for one automatic upload attempt. Connecting does not automatically upload your past activities. To send an older activity, open its details in Endravo and choose Upload to Strava.

Automatic and manual uploads share duplicate protection. If an upload is still processing, use the status check shown on the activity. If it failed, read the message and use Retry only when offered. Endravo stops automatic retries after a failed attempt.

An activity is missing or an upload failed

Open the activity’s Strava section in Endravo and read its upload status. Use View on Strava when available to inspect the result. If the outcome is uncertain, check your Strava activities before trying another upload method.

If permissions have changed, review the connection in Profile. Contact Endravo support with the activity date, the visible error, and your app version if it still fails. Please do not send tokens or passwords.

Edit the caption on an uploaded activity

Open the activity’s Strava section, edit the caption, and save. For an activity already uploaded by Endravo, Save caption to Strava updates that activity rather than creating another upload. Strava may restrict edits based on permissions and activity privacy; Endravo will show an error if an edit is not permitted.

Disconnect or withdraw permission

In Endravo, go to Profile → Data & Permissions → Strava → Disconnect Strava. You can also revoke Endravo’s access from Strava’s My Apps settings.

Disconnecting stops future authorized access. It does not delete activities already uploaded to your Strava account; manage those directly in Strava. For removal of connection-related data held by Endravo, use the deletion contact below.

Everyday Endravo help

Account, backup, or missing gear

Check the account shown in Profile and sign in to the same Endravo account used when saving your data. Different sign-in methods can lead to different accounts. If shoes or workout assignments are missing, contact support with your account email and the affected activity dates before deleting or recreating them.

Health permissions, workouts, and recovery

Check Endravo’s permissions in Apple Health and allow the data you want to use. Keep your iPhone and Apple Watch connected while workouts sync. If metrics are missing, include the workout date and a screenshot of the affected section in your support request.

Routes and offline maps

Download the map area before leaving reliable coverage and confirm it is available on your Apple Watch. For a route or map problem, tell us the area, whether it was offline, and what happened when zooming or navigating.

Subscriptions and billing

For an App Store subscription, manage or cancel it through Apple’s subscription settings. For Apple purchase or refund questions, visit Apple’s purchase support. Contact Endravo for app access issues.

Data access, deletion & contact preferences

Email info@osvaldolopez.us with your Endravo account email and whether you want access, correction, account deletion, or deletion of Strava-related data. We may need to verify account ownership. Never include your password.

For a Strava-data deletion request, we will confirm in writing when deletion is complete. Disconnecting does not remove your original Endravo workouts or delete the copies already in Strava; say explicitly if you also want your Endravo account and workouts deleted.

Tell us your preferred reply address and whether you want replies limited to this support request. We use the information you send to handle your request. Read the Endravo Privacy Policy for more details.
